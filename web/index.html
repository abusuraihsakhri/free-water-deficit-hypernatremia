Free-water deficit

Estimate total body water, free-water deficit to a chosen sodium target, and the Adrogué–Madias sodium change for one litre of infusate.

Estimation only — not a fluid prescription. Treat hypovolaemia and the underlying cause as clinically indicated. The model excludes ongoing renal, gastrointestinal and insensible losses, fluid gains, dynamic renal handling, and sodium correction timing. Check serum sodium serially; do not use this alone for clinical decisions.

Defaults are 0 mmol/L Na and K (e.g., D5W after glucose metabolism). These are not infusion orders.

Calculation detail

The per-litre estimate is not a linear infusion-volume prediction or safe rate recommendation. Actual sodium responses may differ materially.

Enter clinical measurements and select Calculate estimate to see the calculation.

Equations: TBW = weight × TBW fraction; water deficit = TBW × (measured Na / target Na − 1); estimated ΔNa/L = (infusate Na + infusate K − measured Na) / (TBW + 1).

Scope: Adult estimates only. In obesity, severe water depletion, pregnancy, oedema, and critical illness, these TBW fractions may be inaccurate. A clinical assessment and repeated laboratory measurements take precedence.
